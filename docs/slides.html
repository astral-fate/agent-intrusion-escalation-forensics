The Problem

The received diagnosis sends the work in the wrong direction

Every public account attributes the outcome to detection — the campaign was not seen soon enough. That diagnosis decides what gets built next, and the two readings diverge completely.

If detection was the constraint

More sensors. Better classifiers. Lower false-negative rates. A well-funded, well-understood programme — and none of it would have changed this outcome.

If escalation was the constraint

The sensors were already sufficient. What failed is how a correct verdict is ranked and routed — and nothing in the field measures that.

  • The failure is invisible to the metrics. Alert screening is formalised over a binary label space {malicious, benign} with three failure quantities — false-positive rate, uninvestigated rate, decision-quality degradation. Each is satisfied by ŷ = y, so a correctly classified alert ranked below the paging threshold scores as a success on every one.
  • Process safety has what security lacks. EEMUA 191 and ANSI/ISA-18.2 publish a target alarm-priority distribution — 80 % low, 15 % medium, 5 % high — and the ASM Consortium measured 37 real operator consoles against it.
  • The checkable claim. Security publishes no target distribution and no population study of deviation from one. Priority is studied; a standard against which a priority can be called wrong is not.

Not claimed: that Hugging Face's alarm configuration departs from 80/15/5. That configuration is unpublished. The comparison is between two research literatures, not between an organisation and a standard.

Methodology · System Architecture

A manuscript that cannot contradict its own artifacts

Provenance travels with the data

Class — primary, independent, secondary — is attached to the source, not remembered. An upstream revision surfaces as a digest mismatch rather than a silent change.

No number is typed by hand

The manuscript is a template of placeholders. It holds no numbers of its own, so it cannot disagree with the data — though that is consistency, not correctness.

The source bytes are not redistributed

The manifest ships each source's URL, byte count, fetch time and SHA-256; a re-fetch yielding a different digest is reported, not accepted.

Escalation — both halves primary

A verbatim admission from the victim, and two anchored timestamps in OpenAI's own appendix. Neither half depends on press reporting, so a reader who rejects all secondary sourcing keeps this finding intact.

Extent vs. onset

Three dense phases carry 16,074 of 16,521 labelled actions and begin within 1.8 % of campaign time of one another; the last does not begin until 62.1 %. The record is ordered in onset and coextensive in extent — which defeats stage-exclusive response, not stage-keyed response.

The negative result

Mean overlap 0.998 and coextensivity 96.3 % were originally the headline. They are not findings: the spans alone confine them in advance to [0.984, 1] and [93.5 %, 96.8 %].

Envelopes are not duty cycles: the published summary gives only the two marginals of a phase-by-day table whose interior is not public, so simultaneous extent is established here and simultaneous activity is not.

Conclusion

The step from a correct verdict to a human interrupt is the one nobody measures

What the record establishes

At the victim, signals fired and were correlated into a coherent attack signal that was never raised to a criticality triggering on-call. At the perpetrator, a true-positive alert preceded the response by 5 h 34 min. Detection was not the binding constraint at either organisation.

What we propose

EMR — an escalation-miss rate conditioned on a locally defined threshold and reported with its dual — plus an annotation of (consequence class, response window) that transfers between deployments. The adoption cost is annotation, not modelling.

What we release

The corpus, the pipeline and the verification harness, so the same measurement can be run on the next such record. Publishing the phase-by-day interior would cost no disclosure and convert our extent bound into a real duty-cycle measurement.
